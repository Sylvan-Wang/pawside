## 4. 帖子正文（英文）

**标题**

> I read hundreds of reviews of fitness apps. Almost none of them were about features.

**正文**

> I'm building Pawside, a small app for people new to lifting. Before deciding what to build next, I spent a few weeks reading public reviews and forum threads about the big apps: Strong, Hevy, Fitbod, MyFitnessPal, MacroFactor.
>
> I expected feature requests. What I got was mostly one thing: **people quit, and they told you exactly when.**
>
> **What people praised**
> - "Last time's numbers are right there, I don't have to think." This came up again and again for the lifting apps.
> - Open it, start. No setup on the day.
> - Basic logging that stays free.
>
> **What made people leave**
> - Basic logging moved behind a paywall. Some people switched apps over it.
> - Logging something and then not being able to fix it, so they deleted it and started over.
> - Loud celebration screens and streak pop-ups. Several people said a quiet badge felt better than a confetti screen.
>
> None of this is "add more features." It's "make the boring part lighter, and don't punish me for a bad week."
>
> **What that changed for us**
>
> The plan in Pawside moves forward when you train, not by the calendar. You open the app, and the next workout in your split is already there, with the target for each set. If you only have 30 minutes, you pick 30 (or 45, 60, 90) and the session is sized to that. Skip a week and it's still waiting where you left off. There's no streak counter, and the weekly goal is a number you set yourself.
>
> What we still haven't solved: showing last time's numbers next to each set. Right now the weight field is filled with today's *target*, not what you actually did last time. Reading those reviews moved that to the top of our list.
>
> **Caveat:** Pawside started as a Chinese-language app. The English version is new, and the written notes you get after each session are still Chinese only.
>
> If you've built a habit or fitness product: what's the one thing you removed that made people stay longer?
